HOW IT WORKS

Order simply.
We'll confirm the details.

Send an order request and choose how you would like to receive it. Our store will call you to confirm availability and details before dispatch.

Choose your products

Tell us what you need and the quantity. Product availability and pricing are confirmed by the store.

Select pickup or delivery

Choose store pickup or request home delivery. If you choose delivery, share your address for coordination.

Wait for our phone confirmation

Our store will call you to confirm the order, availability, total and fulfilment details. Your request is not confirmed until this call.

Choose a payment method

Pay by cash or scan the store's QR code. For QR payments, our team manually verifies the payment before marking the order as paid.

Store pickup

Collect your order from the store after your order has been confirmed by phone.

Home delivery

Our team will coordinate delivery details with you after confirming the request.

Important: An online order request does not mean the order is automatically accepted, paid or dispatched. Please wait for the store's call.
